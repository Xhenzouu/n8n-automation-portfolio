# Document Requirements

For reservation, buyers must submit:
- Two valid government-issued IDs
- Completed Reservation Agreement
- Proof of billing address
- Signed Data Privacy Consent form

For bank financing review (submitted within 30 days of reservation):
- Latest 3 months payslips or 2 years ITR (for employed buyers)
- Latest 12 months bank statements
- Latest 2 years financial statements (for self-employed buyers)
- Certificate of Employment with compensation details
- Completed loan application form

For Contract to Sell signing:
- Notarized Reservation Agreement
- Proof of down payment installments paid
- Marriage certificate (if applicable)

For turnover:
- Signed Contract to Sell
- Full payment or bank loan release
- Authority to Occupy form

Documents can be submitted digitally via email or in person at the sales office.
