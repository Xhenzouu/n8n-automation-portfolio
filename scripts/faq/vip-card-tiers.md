# VIP Card Tiers

AF Homes offers three VIP membership tiers: Silver, Gold, and Platinum.

**Silver** — issued upon reservation of any unit. Entitles the holder to priority viewing schedules, a dedicated sales contact, and 5% off selected add-ons (parking slots, storage units).

**Gold** — issued upon full down payment. Includes all Silver benefits plus 10% off add-ons, free annual unit inspection, and invitation to exclusive launch events.

**Platinum** — issued upon full payment or referral of three completed reservations. Includes all Gold benefits plus 15% off add-ons, complimentary unit turnover cleaning, and a lifetime priority line for service requests.

VIP cards are non-transferable and tied to the buyer's account. Benefit tiers are evaluated annually and may be upgraded based on account activity.
