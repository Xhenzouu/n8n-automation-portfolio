# Viewing Schedule

Property viewings at AF Homes are available Tuesday through Sunday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed on Mondays for site maintenance.

Walk-in viewings are welcome at the showroom, but priority is given to scheduled appointments. To schedule, contact the sales team with your preferred date, time, and unit type. Confirmations are sent within 24 hours.

For model unit tours, appointments are required at least 2 days in advance.

For site visits to construction areas, appointments are required at least 5 days in advance, and visitors must sign a safety waiver and wear provided protective equipment.

Virtual viewings via video call are available on request for overseas buyers or those unable to visit in person. Same booking process applies.
