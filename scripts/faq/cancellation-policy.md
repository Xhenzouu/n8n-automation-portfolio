# Cancellation Policy

Reservation fees are non-refundable under all circumstances.

For cancellations before Contract to Sell signing, down payments made are refundable less a 10% administrative fee, processed within 60 days.

For cancellations after Contract to Sell signing but before turnover, the standard Maceda Law applies: buyers with at least 2 years of payments are entitled to a 50% refund of total payments made; buyers with less than 2 years forfeit all payments to the developer.

Cancellations must be submitted in writing to the sales office. Verbal cancellations are not honored.

Refunds are issued via the same payment method used for the original transaction, or via check if the original method is no longer available.

For payment-related disputes, contact the billing department before initiating cancellation proceedings.
