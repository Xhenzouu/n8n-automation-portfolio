# General Questions

**When was AF Homes founded?** AF Homes was established in 2015 and has delivered over 3,000 residential units across Metro Manila and surrounding provinces.

**What unit types are available?** Studio, 1-bedroom, 2-bedroom, and 3-bedroom configurations in mid-rise and high-rise developments.

**Where are projects located?** Primary developments are in Makati, Pasig, Quezon City, and Cavite. Upcoming projects include sites in Laguna and Bulacan.

**Do you offer furnished units?** Select units are available semi-furnished (kitchen countertops, cabinets, split-type AC). Full furnishing is available as a paid add-on.

**Is parking included?** Parking is sold separately. Allocation is on a first-come, first-served basis at reservation.

**What payment methods are accepted?** Bank transfer, check, and online payment via accredited payment partners.

**Are pets allowed?** Yes, with registration and adherence to the community pet policy.
