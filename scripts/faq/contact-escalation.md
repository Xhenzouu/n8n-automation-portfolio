# Contact Escalation

Standard inquiries receive a response within 24 hours during business days.

For urgent matters, contact the customer service hotline during business hours (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday).

If an inquiry is not resolved within 48 hours, escalate to the Sales Manager. Escalations must reference the original inquiry number.

For unresolved matters after 7 days, escalate to the Customer Relations Office via email. Include the full email thread and any relevant transaction numbers.

For legal matters or contract disputes, AF Homes routes inquiries to the Legal Department. Response time is 5-7 business days.

VIP cardholders have a dedicated priority line that bypasses standard escalation tiers.
