# Reservation Process

To reserve a unit at AF Homes, submit a completed Reservation Agreement along with a reservation fee equal to 2% of the unit price. Reservation fees are non-refundable but deductible from the down payment.

Upon receipt, our sales team issues a Reservation Confirmation and a schedule for the down payment installments. The buyer then has 30 days to submit all documentary requirements for bank financing review, or to confirm intent to pay in cash.

Once the down payment reaches the agreed threshold, AF Homes prepares the Contract to Sell for signing. The buyer then proceeds to bank financing, or pays the balance in full within the agreed period.

Standard turnaround time from reservation to Contract to Sell is 45-60 days, depending on document completeness and bank processing.
