# Down Payment Terms

Standard down payment terms at AF Homes are 20% of the unit price, payable over 18 months with no interest. Buyers can request shorter or longer terms at reservation, subject to approval.

A 2% reservation fee is deducted from the total down payment. The remaining 18% is divided equally across the payment period. Payments are due on the 5th of each month; a 3-day grace period applies before late fees are assessed.

Buyers who complete their down payment early receive a 2% rebate on the total amount paid.

The remaining 80% balance is settled either through bank financing or cash payment at turnover. Bank financing is arranged by the buyer with an accredited partner bank; AF Homes provides assistance with documentation but does not guarantee approval.
